# Surface brief — SERENDIPIA app shell and its three surfaces

Scope: the whole app shell plus the dictionary home, the position detector, and the theory surface. Visitor mode: Operate.

Audience, job, action: one guitarist at a desktop with the guitar in hand; the job is to turn a chord name into playable positions and sound fast, then carry chords into a progression. Primary action: type or pick a chord and hear it; secondary: punch chords into the progression strip and run it. Theory depth is user-controlled, from beginner to advanced, never assumed.

Proof/content: factual music data authored in-app (chord names, voicings, intervals, progressions). No commercial claims. Audio is self-hosted guitar samples with a synthesized fallback.

Constraints: desktop-first responsive; keyboard-first, no mouse-only path; the Riso Studio world with its own palette and turquoise as accent ink; no cards-of-diagrams SaaS language, no gamification, no dashboard chrome.

Memorable moment: the progression strip advancing sheet by sheet under an ink wipe while the current chord overprints in turquoise.

Unresolved: deploy target; capo; alternate-tunings UI; metronome; export/share.

## Direction contract

THESIS: SERENDIPIA is a music print shop, not a chord website: every chord is a poster, the fretboard is an inked plate, voicings are stacked proofs, and the progression is a strip of chained sheets that advances as it plays; overprint marks the notes two neighbouring chords share, and it refuses the category's card list of diagrams.

OWN-WORLD: Paper #F4F1EA ground, ink black #1A1A1A, riso blue #0078BF, riso orange #FF6C2F, riso yellow #FFE800, turquoise #40E0D0 as accent ink, registration marks at plate corners, print grain over everything, overprint where inks meet. Component language: bordered plates with registration corners, ink-stamp keys, paper sheets with cut lines, mono readouts on ink blocks, hairline ink rules.

STORY: The visitor types a chord and the shop sets it: the name in monumental ink, the plate showing the inked positions, the proof stack for every voicing, the strip for the progression; pressing run advances the sheets under an ink wipe and overprints the chord being heard; the suggestion slips past the strip name what can follow and why.

FIRST VIEWPORT: One print sheet at desktop width: a shop header strip (mark, tabs as ink stamps, run/stop, tempo on a yellow ink block, loop, timbre, notation, language, volume); at left the chord field and the monumental chord name with its Latin reading and mono fact chips; at center the inked fretboard plate with registration marks; below it the stacked voicing proofs; at right the root/quality stamps and the suggestion slips; across the bottom the progression strip. Primary action is the chord field, focused on load.

FORM: Riso print studio (flat spot inks, overprint, grain, registration), position 4 of 7 grounded directions; seed key 06d8a1d9.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
